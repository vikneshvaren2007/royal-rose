The Alchemy of Flavor

♛

True luxury cannot be rushed. Our time-honored three-phase alchemy harmonizes botanical essence with farm-fresh velvet cream.

01

Dawn Harvest

Our heirloom Damask roses are hand-picked at first morning light, precisely when petal oils and aromatic floral nectar reach their peak concentration.

02

Slow Distillation

Using traditional copper Deg-Bhapka hydrothermal distillation, the soul of each petal is gently captured drop-by-drop without chemical solvents.

03

The Velvet Symphony

The pure floral essence is folded into slow-chilled, rich whole milk, creating the signature silky texture and lingering fragrant finish that defines ROYAL.
